What is Dinner at Dee Dee's

Hi, Dee Dee here. I am a Registered Associate Nutritionist from UCL. I often find myself stuck in a monotonous routine and forgetting to appreciate the little joys in life. Dinner at Dee Dee's combines my passion for food and hosting, transforming them into an unforgettable evening. My wish is that these evenings will be filled with smiles, home-cooked meals, and a never-ending flow of wine.

I believe that it is those around us who shape us into who we are today. The friends that would gladly listen to your concerns for the first or the hundredth time, the friends that witnessed your happy and sad tears and supported you through no matter what. If you're reading this, this is a celebration of you.

For friends. By friends.
