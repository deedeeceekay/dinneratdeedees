2025 Trend Predictions

22 Jan 2025 · Written by Chompak Koonnawarote

Happy 2025!

With the New Year fast approaching, there are so many things I look forward to: graduation, adulthood and not to mention- I’m turning 25 (eek!) One can only guess what the next random niche trend will be. These are my predictions for hosting and decorating in 2025. Let’s see if any of them comes true!

1. Personalised (little) touches

Matchboxes, napkins, wax seals. The smaller the item, the better.

2. Spatial table scaping

Foods displayed at various heights that is.

3. Cobalt blue

I’ve been seeing a lot of bright blues lately, it makes for such an eye-catching pop of colour in this gloomy London weather.

4. Manifestation Boards

This is something I’ve never done before but I believe it serves as both an inspiration and a reminder in life. Write down your goals and dreams, focus on that and it will come to you.

Finally, I would like to wish everyone the best year ahead. There are multiple goals I have set for the year and I’m sure the same goes for you. I’m glad we can share this positivity and the supportive mindset. Surround yourself with those who you love and love you

Love, Dee Dee
